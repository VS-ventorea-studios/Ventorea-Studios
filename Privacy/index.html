Your data. Your choices.

This policy explains the types of information that may be processed when you visit the site, sign in, use Ventorea AI, or send feedback.

EXPLORE

A practical guide to how site features and connected services may handle information.

01

Information you provide

When you submit a feedback or support form, the fields you fill in may be sent to the studio’s backend so the message can be received and handled. Only include details needed to explain your request.

02

Google account information

If Google sign-in is enabled and you choose to use it, Google and the configured authentication service process the information needed to verify your account and maintain your session. Enter your password only on the official Google sign-in screen.

03

Profile details

If profile features are available, details such as a display name or bio may be shown according to the feature’s design. Avoid adding personal details you would not want others to see, and do not provide a phone number unless a feature clearly requires it.

04

Ventorea AI

Messages sent to Ventorea AI and its replies are handled by the website backend and may be processed by the configured AI provider to generate responses. Conversation exchanges may also be emailed to the studio support inbox for review, together with the signed-in account email when available. Do not enter passwords, payment details or sensitive personal information. Treat the chat as an online service, not a private vault.

05

Your choices and contact

You can choose not to use optional features and can clear site data through your browser settings, though this may sign you out or reset preferences. For privacy questions, contact ventoreastudios.support@gmail.com. External providers operate under their own policies.

VENTOREA STUDIOS / PRIVACY / YOUR INFORMATION

Your data. Your choices.

This policy describes information handled when you visit the Ventorea Studios website, create an account, use VS AI, upload a file, or contact the studio.

LAST UPDATED: 19 NOVEMBER 2026INDEPENDENT PROJECT
PRIVACY POLICY

Please read this notice before using account, chat, upload, or contact features.

01

Who operates this site

Ventorea Studios is an independently operated project. It is not presented as a registered company. For privacy questions, contact the project at ventoreastudios.support@gmail.com. This notice covers the website at ventoreastudios.online and its features described below.

02

Information you provide

Contact and feedback forms process the details you submit, such as your name, email address, subject, and message, depending on the fields shown. Your account profile may contain a display name, email address, optional phone number, bio, and profile picture. Please provide only information needed for the feature you are using.

03

Google sign-in and account data

If you choose Google sign-in, Google handles its sign-in screen and Supabase Auth manages the website session and authentication records. The website receives account information made available through the authentication flow, such as your email address and display name. Do not share your Google password with Ventorea Studios.

04

Profile pictures

When you upload a profile picture, the image is stored using Supabase Storage. The current upload feature accepts JPG, PNG, and WEBP images up to 20 MB. The avatar storage bucket is configured as public, so a person who obtains the public image URL may be able to view the image. Do not upload an image you expect to remain private.

05

VS AI conversations

Messages sent to VS AI and generated replies pass through the website backend and are sent to Google Gemini to produce a response. Visitors may use the public chat without signing in. Ordinary AI conversations and replies are not emailed to the studio and are not routinely reviewed by staff. When a signed-in user sends a message that matches an automated rule for explicit first-person intent to harm themselves or another person, the system may send a safety alert to the studio support inbox if Resend is configured. The alert contains the flagged message only, the account email, and the time. It does not include the surrounding conversation or the AI reply. Automated detection may miss a concerning message or flag a message incorrectly. This alert inbox is not an emergency response service. Do not submit sensitive information to VS AI.

06

Files sent to AI

The chat feature supports selected file types, including PDF, JPEG, PNG, WEBP, GIF, TXT, CSV, Markdown, and JSON, subject to the limits shown by the upload interface. Files you submit are sent to Google Gemini for analysis. The current upload flow does not save file contents in a Ventorea database. Google processes submitted content under its own terms and data practices. Never upload passwords, identity documents, payment details, private records, or other sensitive information.

07

Browser storage

If “Remember AI chat on this device” is enabled, recent chat messages are stored in your browser using local storage. The current feature keeps up to 12 messages on that device. Other preferences and authentication session data may also be stored by the site or its authentication provider. Clearing browser site data may remove saved chat and preferences or sign you out.

08

Device information and analytics

The device panel reads device type, operating system, browser, screen dimensions, and viewport information in your browser. Optional anonymous analytics, when enabled through the device panel, records a broad device category, operating system, browser, page path, approximate viewport category, and visit time. The analytics record is designed to exclude your IP address, account identity, exact screen resolution, and full user-agent string. You may decline or change the optional analytics choice through the panel.

09

Service providers

The website uses third-party providers to operate features. These currently include Vercel for website hosting and backend functions, Supabase for authentication, database and file storage features, Google for sign-in and Gemini AI processing, and Resend for configured support email notifications. These providers process information as needed to provide their services and under their own terms and policies. Their systems may process information in countries outside Egypt.

10

Why information is used

Information is used to provide website features, authenticate accounts, display profile details, respond to AI requests, analyze files submitted to the AI feature, receive feedback, provide support, maintain security, and understand broad site usage when optional analytics is enabled. The project does not describe these features as a service for emergency, medical, legal, or financial advice.

11

Retention and security

Browser-stored chat and preferences remain on your device until cleared or replaced by the feature. Account records, uploaded profile images, and support emails are handled by the relevant service providers. Retention periods depend on the provider, account state, operational needs, and applicable requirements. No internet service is risk-free. Avoid sending information you would not want processed by the providers listed above.

12

Your requests and choices

You may stop using optional features, decline optional analytics, clear browser storage, or contact the studio to ask about information associated with your account or a message you submitted. Send requests to ventoreastudios.support@gmail.com from the relevant account email where possible. We will review requests and respond where we are able. Some information may remain with providers or be retained where required for security, legal, or operational reasons.

13

Children and young users

The website is not designed to request sensitive personal information from children. If you are under the age required to consent to a feature in your location, use the service with a parent or guardian’s guidance. Do not submit another person’s personal information or images without permission.

14

Changes to this policy

This notice may be updated when website features, providers, or legal requirements change. The date at the top shows the latest revision. Please review this page from time to time.

QUESTIONS?

For privacy requests or questions, email ventoreastudios.support@gmail.com.

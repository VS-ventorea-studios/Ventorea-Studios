ABOUT RSL

More than an open world.

RSL is being designed as a living simulation where transportation, people, economy, weather and everyday systems connect together.

🌎

A Living World

The world is designed to feel active and connected, with NPCs, services, transportation and environmental systems working together.

👥

NPCs

NPCs are intended to have routines, interactions and reactions to events happening around them.

💼

Jobs & Economy

Work, income, spending, services and other economic systems can become part of everyday gameplay.

🌦️

Environment

Weather and environmental conditions are intended to influence the world instead of simply being visuals.

🏙️

Everyday Life

Transportation, shopping, services and normal daily activities can all become part of the simulation.

🎯

Your Choice

RSL is intended to give players freedom without forcing everyone into the same style of gameplay.

“If it exists in real life, it should exist in the game.”
— Ventorea Studios • RSL Design Philosophy
SYSTEMS

Built around simulation.

RSL is planned around interconnected systems instead of treating every feature as an isolated mechanic.

01

🚗 Vehicle Simulation

Vehicles can have individual damage, repairs, handling and consequences from collisions.

02

🛠️ Damage & Repairs

After a crash, the game can identify damaged parts and show what needs to be repaired.

03

👤 NPC Accidents

NPC collisions can create situations involving damage, responsibility and potential compensation.

04

💰 Economy

A planned economy can connect jobs, income, spending and services.

05

🚦 Transportation

Roads, traffic and transportation are planned as important parts of the world.

06

🌦️ Dynamic Weather

Environmental conditions can affect how the world looks and potentially how it behaves.

07

🏠 Everyday Activities

Everyday services and activities can become interactive parts of the world.

08

🎨 Customization

Players are planned to have control over their character, vehicles and other parts of their experience.

VEHICLES

Drive differently.

Vehicles are intended to feel like part of the simulation, not disposable tools.

🚘

Original Vehicles First

Early versions can use fictional vehicle designs so development isn't dependent on expensive licensing.

💥

Realistic Damage

Crashes can damage individual components and provide information about what needs repair.

🔧

Repair System

Damaged vehicles are intended to require repairs rather than simply resetting after an accident.

GRAPHICS

Your hardware. Your choice.

The planned PC system can recommend a graphics preset based on hardware. Console players receive a stable default configuration.

🔴 Low

Designed for lower-end hardware.

🟡 Medium

Balanced visual quality and performance.

🟢 High

Higher visual quality for stronger systems.

🔵 Ultra

Maximum planned visual quality.

⚠️ Performance protection

On consoles, the game is planned to use stable graphics settings by default. If demanding settings become available, a warning can appear before confirmation.

01 / THE IDEA

Not just a place
to play.
A place to belong.

A city should be more than a beautiful backdrop. It should feel like it has a life of its own.

The moments between big events are where a world earns its personality: a familiar route home, a shopkeeper you recognize, a sudden change in the weather, or a conversation that feels different because of what happened yesterday.

Ventorea Studios is an independent game studio focused on thoughtful world-building, connected gameplay, and simulations where everyday choices can create stories worth remembering.

What is Ventorea Studios?＋

An independent game studio building ambitious interactive worlds, starting with Realistic Simulation Life (RSL).

Can I play RSL right now?＋

RSL is in development. There is no publicly confirmed playable release at this time.

Will RSL have multiplayer?＋

Online features are part of the project’s exploration, but exact multiplayer features and availability have not been confirmed.

How much will RSL cost?＋

Pricing and editions have not been announced. We will share confirmed details through official studio channels.

About the Game

A life simulation built around realism, freedom, and a world that continues to live around you.

Our goal is to create a living virtual world where players can experience everyday life while having the freedom to create their own story. The world is designed around interconnected systems, meaning the actions of players and the world itself can affect what happens around them.

Live Your Own Life

Create your own character and build a life your way. Choose where you live, what you do, where you work, and how you spend your time.

Build a Career

Start from the beginning and work your way toward your goals. Build a career, earn money, manage your finances, and create your own future.

Own Property

Buy homes, apartments, businesses, and other properties as you progress through your life.

Explore the World

Travel through cities and different environments, use transportation, visit airports, and experience a world designed to feel connected.

Living Systems

The planned world includes interconnected systems such as jobs, education, transportation, weather, finances, emergency services, law enforcement, and other aspects of everyday life.

Dynamic NPCs

NPCs are planned to behave as part of the world, creating interactions and situations that can change depending on what happens around them.

What Is The Goal?

There is no single way to play.

The goal is to create your own life. You decide what you want to become, how you want to live, and what choices you make along the way. Whether you want to build a successful career, own businesses and property, travel the world, or simply experience everyday life, the choice is yours.

Learn More

Take a closer look at the vision behind Ventorea's life simulation experience.

Ventorea's main game is being designed as a connected virtual world where everyday life, player decisions, and world systems can influence one another. The goal is not simply to create a game where you complete missions. The goal is to create a world where you can decide what kind of life you want to live.

Character & Identity

Players will be able to create and develop their own characters and shape their lives through personal choices, careers, relationships, activities, and progression.

Careers & Economy

The game is planned to include careers, income, spending, finances, businesses, property, and other systems designed to create a connected economic experience.

Cities & Transportation

Players will be able to explore cities and environments while using different forms of transportation. Airports and travel systems are also part of the planned world.

NPCs & World Activity

NPCs are planned to form part of the living world, with activities and behaviors that can change depending on the environment and events around them.

Weather & Environment

Weather and environmental conditions are planned to be part of the world and may interact with different systems and activities.

Emergency Services

Emergency services and law enforcement are planned parts of the world, helping create a more believable and interconnected environment.

Multiplayer

Multiplayer is currently planned. Specific multiplayer features, player limits, and technical requirements have not been finalized.

Continuous Development

Ventorea Studios plans to continue developing the game as the project grows. New systems, features, improvements, and additional content may be added throughout development and after release.

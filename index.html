01 / Studio

Building worlds that feel alive.

Ventorea Studios focuses on realistic interactive experiences where systems, people, cities, and everyday life work together.

“The step before reality.”

Our goal is to create experiences where the world does not feel like a collection of missions. It feels like a place that continues to exist around you.

02 / Main Project
RSL
REALISTIC SIMULATION LIFE

If it exists in real life, it should exist in the game. RSL is an open-world life simulation built around realistic systems, transportation, cities, careers, relationships, and everyday life.

03 / Features

A world that reacts.

RSL is designed around connected systems that affect how people and the world behave.

🧠

NPC Life & Memory

NPCs have needs, relationships, routines, and memory.

💼

Jobs & Economy

Jobs, businesses, taxes, insurance, and everyday finances.

🚆

Transportation

Roads, public transport, airports, airlines, ships, and cruises.

🌦️

Dynamic World

Weather and day-night cycles affect how the world looks and behaves.

👮

Law & Police

Speed enforcement, police interactions, wanted levels, and prison systems.

🏫

Education

Schools and education form part of the wider life simulation.

📱

Personal Devices

Phones, watches, wireless earbuds, charging, and connected personal technology.

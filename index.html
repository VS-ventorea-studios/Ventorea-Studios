01 / Studio

Building worlds around real life.

Ventorea Studios focuses on realistic interactive experiences. Our goal is to create systems that connect together and make digital worlds feel alive.

"If it exists in real life, it should exist in the game."

Quality over quantity. Every system should have a purpose, interact with other systems, and contribute to the world.

02 / Main Project
RSL
Realistic Simulation Life

RSL is an open-world life simulation built around realistic systems, transportation, cities, careers, relationships, vehicles, weather, and everyday life.

View Systems View Pricing
03 / Systems

A world built from systems.

RSL connects different parts of life together instead of treating each feature as an isolated mechanic.

🚗

Realistic Driving

Traffic rules, AI drivers, fuel, maintenance, weather, manual and automatic driving.

🏙️

Living Cities

Large connected cities with transportation, businesses, services, and everyday activity.

👥

NPC Relationships

NPCs have relationships, memories, routines, and reactions to the world around them.

💼

Jobs & Careers

Build a career, work different jobs, run businesses, and manage your finances.

🚇

Transportation

Metro, trains, trams, buses, airports, airlines, ships, and cruises.

🌦️

Dynamic World

Weather and day-night cycles affect how the world looks and behaves.

👮

Law & Police

Speed enforcement, police interactions, wanted levels, and prison systems.

🏫

Education

Schools and education form part of the wider life simulation.

📱

Personal Devices

Phones, watches, wireless earbuds, charging, and connected personal technology.

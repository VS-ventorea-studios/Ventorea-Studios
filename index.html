04 / Features

Systems create stories.

Each system exists to create situations players remember.

🧠

NPC Memory

A shop owner in Cairo remembers you did not pay last time. Relationships and reputation affect future interactions.

🚗

Realistic Driving

Traffic, signals, weather, public roads, vehicle maintenance, and manual or automatic driving shape every trip.

💼

Jobs & Economy

Work, businesses, taxes, insurance, and personal finances connect daily decisions to your wider life.

🚆

Transportation

Roads, metro systems, airports, airlines, ships, cruises, and local transport link the world together.

🌦️

Living Weather

Weather and time affect traffic, routines, visibility, travel, and the feel of each city.

📱

Personal Devices

Phones, watches, earbuds, charging, messages, and connected services form part of everyday life.

👮

Law & Police

Police interactions, wanted levels, enforcement, CCTV outside private homes, and prison systems support a structured world.

🏫

Education & Life

Schools, careers, relationships, housing, and personal routines help build a longer life path.

🌐

Connected World

Move between cities with transport networks and systems designed to keep the world connected.

05 / World

Four cities. One connected world.

Each city has its own rhythm, transport, architecture, and daily systems.

CAIRO
Egypt

Crowded streets, microbuses, local businesses, dense neighborhoods, and fast-changing traffic.

Unique system: Microbus network

Dubai

Metro connections, business districts, major roads, airports, towers, and high-speed city movement.

Unique system: Metro & business districts

Tokyo

Unique system: Rail punctuality

France

Paris

Unique system: Cafe & social life

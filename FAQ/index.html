01

What is Ventorea Studios?

Ventorea Studios is an independent game studio focused on immersive worlds and connected simulation. Realistic Simulation Life (RSL) is its current flagship concept.

02

Can I play RSL now?

Not yet. RSL is still being developed, and a public playable build has not been announced. We’ll share confirmed news through official studio channels.

03

Will RSL have multiplayer?

Multiplayer and other online features have not been confirmed. We’ll avoid promising specific modes until there is something concrete to share.

04

How much will RSL cost?

There is no official price, edition list, or launch date yet. Any figures shared elsewhere should not be treated as official unless confirmed by Ventorea Studios.

05

How do I contact the studio?

Visit Contact to send a support request or share feedback. Include enough detail for us to understand your question, but never send passwords or payment details.

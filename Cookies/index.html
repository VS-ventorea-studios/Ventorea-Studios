Understand what browser storage does and how to manage it on your device.

01

Essential session storage

Google and Supabase authentication may use cookies or similar storage to maintain your sign-in session.

02

Preferences

The website may store choices such as dismissed notices, motion preferences and AI chat history on your device. You can clear site data in your browser; doing so may reset preferences or sign you out.

03

AI, feedback and support

Messages and form submissions are sent to the configured backend services to provide responses or handle requests. Avoid submitting passwords, payment information or sensitive personal data.

04

Third-party services

Hosting, authentication and AI providers may process technical information needed to provide their services. External links have separate privacy practices.

05

Managing storage

Use your browser's privacy settings to clear cookies and site data. Blocking storage may affect sign-in and preferences.

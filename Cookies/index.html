Cookies & Storage.

This notice explains browser storage used by the website and how to manage it on your device.

LAST UPDATED: 19 NOVEMBER 2026INDEPENDENT PROJECT
COOKIES & STORAGE

Browser storage helps account sessions and selected site features work. The exact items depend on the features you use.

01

Authentication storage

When you sign in, Supabase Auth and the browser may store session information needed to keep you signed in and protect account access. Blocking or clearing this storage may sign you out.

02

Local preferences and chat

The website uses browser local storage for selected preferences. If “Remember AI chat on this device” is enabled, recent chat messages are saved locally, up to the feature’s current limit of 12 messages. This information is stored in your browser, not as a cloud chat history in the Ventorea database.

03

Optional analytics

The device panel offers an optional anonymous analytics choice. If enabled, the site records broad device and browser categories, page path, approximate viewport category, and visit time. The analytics record is designed to exclude IP address, account identity, exact screen resolution, and full user-agent string. You may decline or change your choice through the device panel.

04

Third-party providers

Authentication, hosting, AI, and other features rely on external providers. Those providers may set or access their own cookies or similar technologies as part of their services. Their use is governed by their own privacy and cookie notices.

05

Managing browser data

You may review or clear cookies and site data in your browser settings. Clearing data may remove locally saved chat and preferences or end your sign-in session. Blocking all storage may prevent account and interactive features from working properly.

06

Questions

For questions about browser storage or optional analytics, contact ventoreastudios.support@gmail.com. For details about information handled by the website and its providers, read the Privacy Policy.

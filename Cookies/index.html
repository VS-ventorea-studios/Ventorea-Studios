Cookies & Storage.

This notice explains how the website may use browser storage and how connected services may process technical information.

EXPLORE

Understand what browser storage does and how to manage it on your device.

01

Essential session storage

When you sign in, the authentication system may use browser storage to keep your session active, so you do not need to authenticate on every page.

02

Preferences

Some features may save preferences or recent chat information in your browser so the site feels consistent when you return. Clearing this data can reset those choices or remove locally saved information.

03

AI, feedback and support

AI chats and form submissions are sent to the relevant backend services to process your request. Only share information needed for the conversation, and never submit passwords, payment details or sensitive personal information.

04

Third-party services

The site relies on third-party services for hosting, sign-in and AI features. Those providers may process technical information as part of delivering their service; visiting an external link means their own policies apply.

05

Managing storage

You can review or clear cookies and site data in your browser’s privacy settings. Blocking all storage may affect sign-in, saved preferences and other interactive features.

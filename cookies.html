Cookies & Storage.

LAST UPDATED: 12 OCTOBER 2026 · SIMPLE STORAGE NOTICE

This static website is designed to avoid advertising trackers. It may use browser storage to keep basic demonstration preferences working. Browser storage may be cleared through your browser’s privacy settings.

1. Essential session storage

When you sign in to the public demo account, the site stores a temporary sign-in flag in your browser tab’s session storage. It is used only to unlock the demo AI interface and normally disappears when the tab’s session ends. The demo credentials are public; do not enter a real password or confidential information.

2. Preference storage

If you dismiss this notice, the site stores your choice in local storage on your device so the notice does not appear on every visit. The choice is a local browser preference, not an advertising cookie. Use the “Cookie settings” option in the site footer to reopen the notice.

3. AI and support requests

If you send a message to Ventorea AI, the text you submit is sent to the site’s AI backend and its configured AI provider to generate a reply. Do not submit personal, sensitive, or confidential data. The website script limits the conversation history sent with a request. Please also review the policies of the AI provider and hosting provider.

Submitting a feedback or support form may send the information you type to the website’s configured service. Only provide information needed to describe your request.

4. Analytics and advertising

This website does not intentionally set advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. Hosting infrastructure, embedded third-party resources, or linked services may process basic technical data under their own policies. External links may have different privacy practices.

5. Managing storage

You can clear site data using your browser settings. Clearing session storage signs you out of the demo tab; clearing local storage may cause this notice to appear again. Blocking storage may affect demo account and preference behavior.

6. Changes and contact

This notice may be updated if the site’s technical setup changes. Questions can be sent to ventoreastudios.support@gmail.com.

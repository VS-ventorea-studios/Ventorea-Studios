Privacy Policy.

LAST UPDATED: 14 OCTOBER 2026

This policy explains how Ventorea Studios may handle information when you visit our website, sign in, use Ventorea AI, or submit a feedback or support request. The exact information processed depends on which features you use.

1. Information you provide

If you send a feedback or support form, we process the information you enter, which may include your name, email address, request category, subject, and message. Please provide only information needed to handle your request and avoid sensitive personal information.

2. Account sign-in

If you choose Google sign-in, authentication is handled using Google and Supabase. These services may process information needed to authenticate you and maintain your session, such as account identifiers and authentication tokens. We do not ask you to type your Google password into a Ventorea Studios form.

3. Ventorea AI

Messages you submit to Ventorea AI are sent to the website's backend to generate a response and may be processed by the AI provider configured for that service. Conversation context may be included to support replies. Do not submit passwords, payment information, confidential material, or sensitive personal data. Processing and retention by third-party providers are also subject to their own policies and settings.

4. Browser storage and technical information

The website may use browser storage for session behavior and preferences. Hosting providers and other technical services may process standard connection or diagnostic information needed to deliver and secure the website. See our Cookies & Storage notice for more detail.

5. How information is used

Information may be used to operate the website and its features, respond to feedback or support requests, maintain sign-in, improve reliability, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot technical issues.

6. Service providers and sharing

Information may be processed by providers that help operate the site, including hosting, authentication, form-handling, and AI services. We do not claim that information is never shared with providers necessary to deliver a feature. We do not intentionally use the website to sell personal information or serve behavioral advertising.

7. Retention and security

Retention depends on the feature and the systems involved. We use services and reasonable measures intended to protect information, but no website or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. Avoid sending information you would not want processed by the relevant service providers.

8. Your choices and requests

You can choose not to use sign-in, AI chat, or forms. You can clear browser storage through your browser settings. To ask about personal information in a feedback or support request, or to request account-related assistance, contact us using the email below. We may need enough information to locate and verify the relevant request.

9. Children and age

This website is not intended to encourage anyone to submit sensitive personal information. If you are a parent or guardian with a concern about information submitted by a child, contact us so we can review the request.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the website, providers, or features change. The date at the top indicates the latest revision.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, contact ventoreastudios.support@gmail.com.

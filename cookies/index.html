Cookies & Storage.

LAST UPDATED: 14 OCTOBER 2026 · SIMPLE STORAGE NOTICE

This website may use browser storage for sign-in session behavior and preferences. This notice explains the main uses. The exact data processed can also depend on hosting and third-party services you use.

1. Essential session storage

When you sign in, the site and its authentication provider may use session storage, cookies, or similar mechanisms to maintain your session and protect sign-in. The exact storage depends on the configured authentication flow. Do not share your device or leave an account session open on a shared device.

2. Preference storage

The website may save choices such as whether you dismissed a notice so that it does not appear on every visit. You can clear browser storage through your browser's privacy settings; doing so may reset preferences or sign you out.

3. AI, feedback and support

Messages sent to Ventorea AI are sent to the site's backend and may be processed by its configured AI provider. Feedback or support forms send the details you submit to the configured service so your request can be handled. Avoid sending passwords, payment information, confidential material, or sensitive personal data.

4. Analytics, advertising and technical data

The site is not intentionally designed to serve behavioral advertising. Hosting infrastructure and third-party services may process technical information needed to deliver, protect, and troubleshoot their services. External links have their own privacy practices.

5. Managing browser storage

You can clear cookies and site data using your browser settings. Blocking storage may affect sign-in, preferences, or other website features.

6. Changes and contact

This notice may be updated when the website or its services change. Questions can be sent to ventoreastudios.support@gmail.com.
